Matt Sumegi

Lead Developer of TableTrade

Matt Sumegi is the Lead Developer of TableTrade, a product of Central Alberta Technologies.

TableTrade is the in-the-room Pokémon TCG tool he built for a phone at a real card shop: have-list, want-list, scan or search, and a ping when someone at this table is a match. Trade here, now.

He is a town councillor in Castor, Alberta.
