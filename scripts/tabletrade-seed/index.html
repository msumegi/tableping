What it does

  • 01

    Have-list and want-list

    What you can trade right now. What you are hunting. Lives on the phone.

  • 02

    Scan or search

    Point the camera at one Pokémon card, or type a name / set. Confirm the printing. Flip to the next.

  • 03

    Same table, same room

    Share a four-character table code or scan a QR across the felt. You stay at the same table.

  • 04

    A ping when it is a match

    They want a card you have, you want a card they have, or both. Full-screen ping. Then you talk.

  • 05

    Shop-scale. Pokémon only.

    About a room away (~120 m). Match here, then talk.

Here. This room. This table.

Someone sitting next to you has the printing you need. TableTrade is the tap that finds that out now — so you trade here.

  1. Open it at the table

    Browser or home-screen PWA. You are already at the shop.

  2. Load the lists

    Add a few haves and wants. Scan a stack one card at a time, or search Pikachu and tap the printing.

  3. Join the table

    One phone shows a QR or table code. The other scans or types it. Optional: tap “I’m at the shop” for room-scale GPS.

  4. The ping

    If the lists overlap, both phones fire. You are already here — trade now.
