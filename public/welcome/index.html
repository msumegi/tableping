What it does

  • 01

    Have-list and want-list

    Cards you want to trade away, and cards you want. They stay on your phone.

  • 02

    Scan or search

    Point the camera at one Pokémon card, or type a name. Confirm the set and number. Flip to the next.

  • 03

    Check in

    Press Check in when you’re at a shop or event. You’ll get a ping if your lists match someone nearby.

  • 04

    A ping when the lists match

    They want a card you have, you want a card they have, or both. Then meet up and trade.

Get started.

Someone nearby may have the card you want, or want a card you have. Check in, and both phones ping. Then meet up and trade.

  1. Open it

    Use it in the browser, or add it to your home screen.

  2. Load the lists

    Add cards you want to trade away and cards you want. Scan one card at a time, or search Pikachu and pick the set and number.

  3. Check in

    Press Check in when you’re at a shop or event. Keep the app open.

  4. The ping

    If your lists match, both phones ping. Then meet up and trade.
