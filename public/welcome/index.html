What it does

  • 01

    Have-list and want-list

    What you’d trade. What you’re hunting. Lives on the phone.

  • 02

    Scan or search

    Point the camera at one Pokémon card, or type a name. Confirm the printing. Flip to the next.

  • 03

    Tap I’m looking

    A match close by buzzes you. Bluetooth in the room. Name, cards, and an optional I’m over here line.

  • 04

    A ping when lists overlap

    They want a card you have, you want a card they have, or both. Then you talk.

Here. Close by.

Someone sitting next to you has the printing you need. TableTrade is the tap that finds that out now, so you trade here.

  1. Open it

    Browser or home-screen. You are already here.

  2. Load the lists

    Add a few haves and wants. Scan a stack one card at a time, or search Pikachu and tap the printing.

  3. Check in

    Tap I’m looking. Bluetooth finds who is close. Keep Bluetooth on. Find them in the room.

  4. The ping

    If the lists overlap, both phones fire. You are already here. Trade now.
