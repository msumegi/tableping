Match at this table tonight.

TablePing is the have-list / want-list phone app that pings you when someone at this table — or in this room tonight — is a trade. Scan or search Pokémon cards. Not a 25-mile marketplace. Not Magic. Not One Piece.

What it does

  • 01

    Have-list and want-list

    What you can trade tonight. What you are hunting. Lives on the phone — no account, no password.

  • 02

    Scan or search

    Point the camera at one Pokémon card, or type a name / set. Confirm the printing. Flip to the next.

  • 03

    Same table, same room

    Share a four-character table code or scan a QR across the felt. Indoors, GPS is often junk — this still works.

  • 04

    A ping when it is a match

    They want a card you have, you want a card they have, or both. Full-screen ping. Then you talk.

  • 05

    Shop-scale. Pokémon only.

    About a room away (~120 m), not a city radius. No meetup scheduler. No other TCGs in v1.

A night at the shop

Friday binder night. Someone across the table is sitting on the exact printing you need. TablePing is the tap that finds that out before you pack up.

  1. Show up with the binder

    Open TablePing in the browser — or from the home screen if you installed the PWA.

  2. Load tonight’s lists

    Add a few haves and wants. Scan a stack one card at a time, or search Pikachu and tap the printing.

  3. Join the table

    One phone shows a QR or table code. The other scans or types it. Optional: tap “I’m at the shop” for room-scale GPS.

  4. The ping

    If the lists overlap, both phones can fire. You are already in the same room — walk over and trade.
